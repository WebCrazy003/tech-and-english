AI models
---------
Models are not part of the app. You download them yourself (Settings › AI and Settings › Voice).
Each model's license is shown before the download, and the downloaded models are listed in
Settings › About.

macOS
-----
The dictionary meanings come from the dictionary built into macOS, and the voices are the
voices installed on your Mac. They are not part of the app.
